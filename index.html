02About Bhilai Trading Co.

Safety is not an option. It is a responsibility.

Established in 2020 in Supela, Bhilai, Bhilai Trading Co. provides fire safety products, industrial fire protection equipment, systems and services — a one-stop sourcing and supply partner for every fire protection requirement.

We are a wholesaler, trader and service provider of fire extinguishers, extinguisher refilling, industrial fire safety equipment, foam and ABC fire extinguishers and fire alarm systems. Our products are manufactured from high-quality materials procured from well-known vendors — and alongside every supply, we provide fire safety training to our clients. Because precaution is better than cure.

  1. 01

    Fire safety equipment supply

    Portable and wheeled extinguishers, accessories and first-response safety equipment for homes, institutions and industry.

  2. 02

    Fire extinguisher refilling

    Refilling and recharging services that keep your existing extinguishers ready for the moment they are needed.

  3. 03

    Industrial fire protection solutions

    Equipment for plants, warehouses and facilities — from DCP and foam trolley units to hydrant fittings and hose systems.

  4. 04

    Fire alarm systems

    Detection and warning devices — smoke detectors, manual call points and alarm equipment for early response.

  5. 05

    Hydrant & sprinkler systems

    Hydrant valves, inlets, hose reels, sprinkler heads and pipeline valves for building protection networks.

  6. 06

    Fire safety awareness & training

    Face-to-face and virtual training on workplace hazards, extinguisher handling and emergency preparedness.
